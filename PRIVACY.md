# Privacy Policy for Pronunciation Helper

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Pronunciation Helper helps you hear the pronunciation of words and short phrases selected on webpages. This policy applies to the Chrome and Firefox extension.

## Website content and local processing

The extension reads your selected text, nearby webpage text, and HTML language declarations to determine the language and display a pronunciation popup. It passes the selected text to your browser's speech synthesis service when you request playback. Language detection uses local heuristics and, when available, the browser's built-in on-device language detector.

The extension does not persist selected text or surrounding content, maintain a browsing history, or send webpage content to servers operated by the developer. Text is used in memory to provide the current pronunciation feature. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or model training by the developer.

## Speech synthesis services

Pronunciation uses the Web Speech API and voices available through your browser and operating system. Some voices run locally; others may use an online speech service. The extension does not restrict playback to local voices. If a nonlocal voice is used, your selected text may be sent to that service and handled under its provider's privacy policy. Service availability and data handling depend on your browser, operating system, and selected voice. The developer does not operate these speech services or receive their processing results beyond playback through the browser.

## Permissions

- Website access allows the extension to detect selections, inspect surrounding text and language declarations, and place its popup on the webpage you are viewing.
- The context menu permission adds the “Pronounce” action for selected text.

These permissions serve the pronunciation feature. The extension does not log your clicks, keystrokes, browsing history, location, or account credentials.

## Storage, sharing, and sale

The extension has no account system, advertising, analytics, tracking cookies, or persistent user-data storage. The developer does not collect, sell, or transfer your data. The browser or operating system may process selected text through a speech provider as described above. Data is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

## Your choices

You choose the text to pronounce and initiate playback with the popup or context menu. You can manage website access, disable the extension, or uninstall it through your browser's extension settings. Avoid requesting pronunciation of sensitive text if you do not want it processed by a potentially online speech service.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the extension's data practices change. The revision date appears above. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through [the project's issue tracker](https://github.com/costinEEST/pronunciation-helper/issues). Issues are public; do not include sensitive personal information.
